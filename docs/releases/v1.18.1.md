## What's changed in v1.18.1

### Indexing and thumbnail progress fix

- Fixed indexing appearing stuck at "N / N" for a long time on large scans: newly-indexed images are now committed before the (potentially slow) sidecar-tag sync phase runs, instead of after.
- Fixed thumbnail generation looping without progress during a large scan: the thumbnail worker can now see newly-indexed images as soon as they are committed, rather than waiting for the entire scan (including sidecar sync) to finish.
- Indexing status now shows "Synchronizing tags: N / M" during the sidecar-tag sync phase instead of appearing frozen.

**Full changelog**: https://github.com/baddonkey/exif-turbo/compare/v1.18.0...v1.18.1
